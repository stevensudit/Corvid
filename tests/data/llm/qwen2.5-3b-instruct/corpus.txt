Qwen2.5 tokenizer corpus for corvid/llm.

This file is a fixture. Its reference encoding lives beside it in
corpus_tokens.json, produced by scripts/llm_oracle/qwen2_oracle.py.
Every line is chosen to exercise one clause of the pre-tokenizer's
split rule where it differs from GPT-2's, or to catch a shortcut that
passes plain English.

Digits split one at a time: 7 42 1969 3.14159 2nd 1,000,000
Letters after one non-letter: (word [word {word "word" 'word' -word +word
Letters after a space keep it: a word, another word
Letters right after a digit: 1st 2nd 3x 4abc x1y2 abc123def
Contractions, any case: it's IT'S It'S they're THEY'RE we've WE'VE I'm I'M you'll YOU'LL he'd HE'D
Not a contraction: 'tis o'clock rock'n'roll
Punctuation run then newlines: Hello!!!

Punctuation run before a letter: ...ok ---end
Whitespace then newline:
Tabs	and	more	tabs
Trailing spaces before text:   indented
Mixed: price $19.99 (tax 8%), ETA 10:30am; ok?
Unicode letters: naïve café über straße
Non-Latin: 日本語 한국어 Русский العربية
Emoji and symbols: 🚀 rocket, © 2024, ½ cup, → arrow
Precomposed accents only, since the engine does no NFC step: é è ê ñ
A sentence ending without a newline
